Wellness

Twenty seconds in the morning

Five quick questions on the phone: sleep, energy, soreness, stress and mood. Each answer is a slide on a grey scale from very badly to very well, with no numbers and no colours, so players answer how they feel, not what score they think the staff want. Staff see who is below their own normal and needs a conversation, not a spreadsheet.
