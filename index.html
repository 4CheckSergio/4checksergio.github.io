Why we built it

Data is only worth what it changes on the ice.

We come from inside the performance department. We know the hours in front of the computer, trying to make sense of it all, and how little is left when it is finally time to coach. Measuring for the sake of data doesn't help the athlete or the coach. What counts is a better decision, made sooner, by people who still have the energy to make it.

Less time on the laptop with better information means more time for the relationships that actually move athletes. And it means evenings at home that belong to your family again.
