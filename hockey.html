Ice hockey · Match Analysis

Built on the ice first.

ForeCheck was built with hockey staff on real season data. Every metric was chosen for skating, shifts and the game calendar, and every one is backed by ice-hockey research.

Skating Load

Volume that counts strides, not wobbles

Daily volume is Skating Load: the force of every skating stride times the player's body mass. Shooting, contact and bench movement don't inflate it, and a heavier player pushing the same stride gets credit for the work he does. It drives the daily load, one Skating Load ACWR across the full width of the Locker Room, and the 7-day forecasts.

Skating Load ACWR · team · 0.8 to 1.3 band

Density (SL/min)

How packed each minute on the ice was

Skating Load per active minute shows how much skating went into each minute a player actually skated. Together with % high-force strides and % high-intensity skating load it makes the session intensity score, against the player's own normal. It has no ratio of its own: it barely moves from day to day, so an ACWR built on it would mostly count how often players skate.

Late-game drop-off

Who fades in the third period

Match Analysis shows every period as a percentage of the same players' own first period, for density and % high-force strides. Both are per minute or per stride, so a period with more or less ice time is still compared fairly.

Played vs did not play

The players who sat out don't fall behind

After a game, ForeCheck sets the load of the players who did not play against those who did and drafts their after-game session, so scratched players and extras keep their conditioning instead of losing a week.

Off-ice detection

Vest on, but on the bike

Players often keep the vest on for the bike. ForeCheck spots pedalling in the vest data, so a bike session can't pass as an easy skate. When the Wattbike file overlaps, it is confirmed automatically; otherwise the coach confirms or denies from one list. Outdoor runs are recognised from the step rate, even without GPS.

Match-day tags

Every session knows how far it is from the game

Sessions are tagged by days to the game, from MD-3 to MD+1. A player's MD-1 practice is compared with his other MD-1 practices, never with a game, so “harder than normal” really means harder than his normal for that day.
