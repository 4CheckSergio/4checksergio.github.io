Swiss hosting

Your athletes' data stays in Switzerland

iMore

ForeCheck runs on a server in Switzerland, with Infomaniak, a Swiss provider. Player data is stored and processed there, under Swiss law and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP).

Where it lives

  • AppCHInfomaniak server in Switzerland
  • BackupCHSwiss Backup, Switzerland

Backups

Encrypted every night, kept for a year

iMore

Every night the data is encrypted and copied to a separate backup in Switzerland. We keep 14 daily, 8 weekly and 12 monthly copies, so a mistake or a lost device never costs you a season.

Who sees what

Health data only for the people who need it

iMore

Every staff member gets a role. The head coach always sees each player's return-to-play stage, and the medical detail behind it can be limited to the physio and doctor whenever the club needs that. Health data such as rehab, heart rate and tests is a separate switch that the club turns on only when it is ready.

Your data

Yours to keep, yours to take with you

iMore

The club owns its data. It is never sold and never used for anyone else's purposes, including training AI. Ask and you get a full export in open formats, so you are never locked in.

Questions clubs ask

Answered before you ask

  • In which country is our data stored?In Switzerland, on Infomaniak servers, and the backups too.
  • Who on the staff can see medical details?You decide. Medical detail can be limited to the physio and team doctor, while the head coach still sees the return-to-play stage.
  • What happens if a laptop is lost?Nothing is lost. The data lives on the server, not on the laptop, and is backed up every night.
  • Can we take our data with us if we leave?Yes. You get a full export in open formats, CSV and JSON.
